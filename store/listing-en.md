## Full description (4000 characters)

```
Kundo is a simple notebook that keeps your day and your money in one place.

THE DAY
Tasks are split into parts of the day: morning, afternoon, evening. Each one
can have a category, a priority and an exact time. Whatever you did not finish
yesterday is not lost — one button moves it to today.

REMINDERS
A task with a time set gets a notification at that hour. You can also turn on
a daily reminder that asks you to tick off tasks and habits at the end of the
day. You choose the time.

REPEATING TASKS
Write a task that repeats every day, or only on weekdays, once — after that it
shows up in the list on its own.

MONEY
An expense or an income takes a couple of seconds to write down. At the end of
the month you can see where the money went: the split by category, a chart by
day, the balance between income and spending, and the change from last month.

BUDGET
Set how much you are willing to spend in a month and the app keeps track of
what is left and how much a day that leaves you.

HABITS
Exercise, reading, water — one tap a day. You can see how many days in a row
you kept it. The monthly trend shows the whole month as one strip: which days
you marked, what percentage you kept, and your best streak.

THE WEEK
The whole week on one screen: which day is full, which one is free.

MAKE IT YOURS
Categories are yours to manage: add new ones, change a name or a colour,
remove the ones you do not need. Deleting a category never deletes its
records — you choose which category they move to. There is a light and a dark
theme, or the app can follow your phone’s setting.

YOUR DATA STAYS WITH YOU
No sign-up. No server. It works offline. Every record is kept on the phone
itself and is never sent anywhere. You can back everything up into a single
file at any time, or export your expenses as a table for Excel.

No ads. No subscription. No permissions it does not need.

Available in Uzbek, Russian and English. The colours and patterns come from
Bukhara tilework.
```
