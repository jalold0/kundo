Support

Help with the Kundo app

Have a question?

If something in the app does not work, or you have an idea, write to jaloldin@buxoro.online. You normally get an answer within a day or two.

When reporting a problem, add your phone model and Android or iOS version — it makes finding the cause much faster.

Frequently asked questions

Can I lose my data?

Every record is kept on your phone. If you change phones or remove the app, the records go with it. So make a copy from time to time through Settings → Back up — then Restore from backup brings everything back on the new phone.

Can I use it on two phones?

There is no automatic sync yet. You can export a backup file on one phone and restore it on the other. Cloud sync is planned and will be optional.

Why is there no sign-up?

That is on purpose. With no account there is no password, no server and no risk of your data leaving you. The app works without internet.

How do I change the app language?

Settings → Language offers three languages: Uzbek, Russian and English. On first launch the app follows your phone's language. Task and category names you typed yourself are not translated — they stay exactly as you wrote them.

Reminders are not arriving

Check two things: whether the switch in Settings → Reminders is on, and whether notifications are allowed for the app in your phone settings. A task reminder only arrives for tasks that have a time set. On some phones battery saving stops background work — excluding Kundo from those restrictions makes reminders arrive on time.

How do I clear finished tasks?

They move to the end of the list on their own and are not carried to the next day. To remove one for good, tap the task and choose “Delete” — if that was a mistake, the “Undo” button at the bottom brings it back.

How do I stop a repeating task?

Open the Habits section — at the bottom there is a “Repeating tasks” list with a “Stop” button.

Can I change the categories?

Yes. In Settings → Categories you add new ones, change a name or a colour, and remove the ones you do not need. Deleting a category never deletes its records — the app asks which category they should move to.

Does the app cost anything?

No. The app is free, with no in-app purchases and no ads.
