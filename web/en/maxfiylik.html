Privacy policy

Last updated: 8 September 2026 · the Kundo app

In short: the app collects, stores and transmits nothing about you — everything you write stays on your device. The only thing kept is the address you yourself leave in the waiting list on this site (details below).

What you type into the app

You can write the following into the app:

  • tasks — the name, time, category and priority;
  • daily habits and the days you marked;
  • money records — the amount, category, date and an optional note;
  • the monthly budget figure;
  • the names of categories you created yourself.

Where that data is kept

All of it is kept in your device's internal storage, in the app's private area. Nothing is sent to a server. The developer cannot see this data and has no access to it.

Data is not shared

The app does not pass data to third parties, advertising networks or analytics services. There are no ads in the app and no tracking of what you do in it.

You are in control

  • Backup: “Settings → Back up” exports all of your data into a .json file. You choose where the file is saved.
  • Export: money records can be exported as CSV (a table).
  • Deletion: “Settings → Delete all data” removes every record for good. Removing the app from the device erases all data as well.

Permissions

The app uses the following system capabilities:

  • Notifications — only if you turn reminders on in the settings yourself. Reminders are scheduled and shown on the device itself; they are not tied to any server, which means they are not push messages. Turning them off in the settings cancels every scheduled reminder.
  • File picker — only when you tap “Restore from backup”, to read the file you pointed at.
  • The share sheet — to save the backup or the CSV wherever you choose.

The app does not touch your location, contacts, camera, microphone or call log.

The waiting list on this site

This site has a “Want to know when it ships?” form. That is part of the site, not of the app. If you leave your email:

  • The address (and your name, if you enter one) is stored in a database on the Neon service. The date it was added is recorded too.
  • The address is used for one message only — on the day the app appears in the stores. No newsletter, no ads, no other mail.
  • The address is never sold, shared or handed to advertising networks.
  • You can leave the list at any time — just write to jaloldin@buxoro.online and your entry will be deleted.

The app itself has nothing to do with this form: it never asks for an email, and the records you keep in the app never reach this database.

Fonts

The fonts inside the app are bundled into the program itself — they are not downloaded from the internet. On this site, however, the fonts are loaded through Google Fonts, so opening the site makes your browser send a request to a Google server.

Children

The app asks nobody for personal data, children included.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated text will be published on this page and the date above will be replaced. If cloud sync is added in the future it will be optional, and this text will be updated beforehand.

Contact
