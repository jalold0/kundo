Small, but enough for every day

Nothing extra. Only what you need every day — and it works well.

The day

A task carries a category, a priority and an exact time. Whatever you did not finish is not lost — one button moves it to today.

Money and budget

Expenses and income, categories you manage yourself, a monthly budget. The app keeps track of what is left and how much a day that leaves you.

Habits

Exercise, reading, water — one tap a day. Days in a row are counted, and the monthly trend shows the whole month as one strip.

Reminders

A task with a time set gets a notification at that hour. The daily reminder asks you to tick off tasks and habits at the end of the day.

Week overview

The whole week on one screen: which day is full, which is free, what is still open.

Backup and export

Export every record into a single file and restore it whenever you want. Expenses can also be exported as a table for Excel.

What the app looks like

The colours and patterns come from Bukhara tilework. Light and dark themes follow your phone's setting. Available in Uzbek, Russian and English.
